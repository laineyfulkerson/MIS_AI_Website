Malware and Cyber Threats

Cybersecurity is important because it helps protect personal information and keeps people safe while using technology. Businesses, schools, and individuals rely on cybersecurity to prevent hackers from accessing private information. Common online threats include phishing emails, weak passwords, and harmful software. People can improve their online safety by creating strong passwords, using two-factor authentication, and avoiding suspicious links. Understanding cybersecurity helps everyone use technology more safely and responsibly.

The WannaCry Ransomware Attack

On May 12, 2017, a major cyberattack called WannaCry ransomware began spreading across the world. The malware locked computer files and demanded payment to restore access, affecting organizations including hospitals and businesses. This attack was significant because it showed how quickly malware could spread and disrupt important services around the world.
